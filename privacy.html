Privacy policy · Effective 6 October 2026

Sana: Voice Tasks is developed by Abhiraman Bakshi. This policy describes how the iPhone app handles your information.

On your iPhone

Tasks, reminder preferences, your name, optional email and optional profile photo are stored locally. Your email is not used to create an account or send marketing. Sana uses the photo you select through Apple’s photo picker.

Voice processing

Microphone audio is recorded locally. Sana requests on-device speech recognition and processes transcripts into tasks using a bundled on-device language model. Audio and transcripts are not sent to a developer-operated server or a cloud AI service. If on-device speech recognition is unavailable, recording cannot be transcribed.

Successful recordings are deleted after transcription. A failed recording may remain locally so you can retry; cancelling or starting a new recording removes the previous recording.

Private iCloud sync

If Backup & sync is enabled, task data is stored in your private iCloud database using Apple CloudKit. We do not have access to your private database. Profile details and recordings are not included in Sana’s task sync. Apple handles iCloud under its own privacy policy.

Purchases & notifications

Apple processes purchases. Sana checks verified StoreKit transactions to determine trial and unlock status; we do not receive payment-card details. Reminders use local iOS notifications and require your permission.

No tracking

Sana has no advertising, tracking SDKs or developer-operated analytics service. We do not sell your data.

Your controls

Edit or delete tasks in the app, change your profile details, disable Backup & sync, or revoke microphone, speech recognition and notification permissions in iOS Settings. To remove Sana’s iCloud data, use Apple’s iCloud storage controls. Removing the app deletes its local app container; a trial-start marker in the iOS Keychain may remain to prevent repeated trials.

Support & this website

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and uses no analytics scripts or cookies added by us. GitHub may process technical connection information under its own privacy policy. Public support issues and anything you include in them are visible on GitHub. Do not submit sensitive information.

Questions & updates

Contact us through Sana support. Updates to this policy will appear here with a revised effective date.
