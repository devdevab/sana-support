A calmer way to get things done.

Sana turns your spoken plans into tasks for Work and Play. Add dates and times, stay close to your day with widgets, and choose colour or monochrome.

Need a hand?

For help, report a problem or ask a question on our support page. Please include your iOS version and what happened. Do not post recordings, private tasks, passwords or personal information: support reports are public.

Contact support

Voice tasks

Tap the microphone and speak naturally. Tap the ribbon to finish, or tap outside it to cancel. Sana transcribes and organises tasks on your iPhone. Microphone and Speech Recognition permissions are required. Finish your phone call before recording.

Reminders

Add a date and time when speaking or typing, or tap the time beneath a task to choose one. Allow notifications in iOS Settings to receive reminders. Reminder preferences and sounds are in Profile.

Widgets & appearance

Add Sana widgets from your Home Screen’s widget gallery. Change between colour and monochrome in Profile.

Trial & purchase

Try Sana free for three days. After the trial, unlock it with a one-time purchase. There is no subscription or automatic charge. Use Restore purchase in Profile to restore an existing unlock with the same Apple Account. Your local App Store shows the applicable price.

Backup & sync

When enabled, Sana syncs tasks through your private iCloud database. Sign in to iCloud on your devices and enable Backup & sync in Profile.
